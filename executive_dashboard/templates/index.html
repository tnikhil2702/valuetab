Intake = one resolved Neos record (status Case or any Rejected status; any record with a Date Opened also counts), counted in the month of Intake Date. Resolved intakes only: unresolved intakes are left out (per Brian), so the latest months may rise as intakes resolve. Case = any record with a Date Opened, counted in the month of Date Opened, any status (per Brian). Office views leave out "Not recorded" (blank office) rows; read cases only by office. Conversion defaults to the method Bradford uses today (cases opened in the period divided by intakes taken in the period, same filters on both). The intake-cohort method is available as the alternative. Spam leads count toward intake totals (Bradford). Percentages on fewer than 25 intakes are marked with an asterisk. Client names are not stored; Case Number is the only identifier kept.
